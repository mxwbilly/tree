Common buyer questions

  • Can we get exclusive designs? Yes — OEM exclusive mold available from MOQ 500 pcs/design. Mold fee quoted separately.
  • Are these waterproof? Resin and MGO are water-resistant. All designs have drainage hole standard. Not suitable for prolonged outdoor flooding.
  • How do you prevent breakage in shipping? Individual gift boxes + foam insert + outer carton bubble-wrap layer. Breakage rate <1% in standard shipping.
  • Seasonal new designs? We release 10–15 new designs per quarter. WhatsApp subscribers receive first-look catalogue.

Freight & export notes

  • Resin designs: lightweight (150–400 g each) — air freight viable for small trial orders.
  • MGO cement designs: heavier — recommend sea freight FCL for orders over 500 pcs.
  • HS 6913.90 (ceramic-look) or 3926.90 (resin) — verify with your customs broker per specific design.
  • FOB Yiwu or FOB Shenzhen depending on factory allocation — quote specifies which port.

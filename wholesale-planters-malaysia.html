FAQ — Wholesale Planters for Malaysia Buyers

What is the minimum order quantity (MOQ) for planters shipped to Malaysia?

MOQ depends on the product: double-layer self-watering planters and creative shaped planters from 50 pcs, transparent orchid pots from 200 pcs, root control and gallon nursery pots from 500 pcs. OEM private label orders with custom branding typically require 20–30% higher MOQ to keep unit cost stable.

How long does shipping from China to Malaysia take?

Sample lead time is 5–10 days via express courier (DHL/FedEx). Bulk production takes 20–30 days, plus approximately 7–10 days sea freight to Port Klang or Penang Port. Total from deposit to Malaysia port is typically 30–45 days. We coordinate with your nominated forwarder or can recommend one.

Can you provide bilingual (BM/EN) labels or Malaysia-specific packaging?

Yes. We can produce bilingual Bahasa Malaysia / English labels, carton markings, and insert cards. Please share your retailer's packaging specifications during the quotation stage so we can confirm feasibility and any additional tooling cost.

What payment terms and Incoterms are available for Malaysia?

Standard terms: TT 30/70 (30% deposit, 70% before shipment). We support FOB Shenzhen/Shanghai and CIF Port Klang or Penang Port. LC at sight available for orders above USD 20,000. We issue full export documentation: commercial invoice, packing list, CO, and BL.

How do I get a fast wholesale price list for Malaysia?

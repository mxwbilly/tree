Why air-pruning matters in SEA

  • Tropical heat: roots grow faster in warm climates — air pruning prevents pot-bound root spiral more effectively than solid-wall pots.
  • Transplant success: air-pruned root balls transplant with far less shock — critical for commercial growers who move plants multiple times.
  • Water drainage: perforated walls prevent waterlogging in high-rainfall regions (Vietnam, Indonesia rainy season).

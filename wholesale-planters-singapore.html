FAQ — Wholesale Planters for Singapore Buyers

What is the minimum order quantity (MOQ) for planters shipped to Singapore?

MOQ depends on the product line: double-layer self-watering planters and creative shaped planters from 50 pcs, transparent orchid pots from 200 pcs, root control and gallon nursery pots from 500 pcs. OEM orders with custom branding typically require 20–30% higher MOQ for cost stability.

How long does shipping from China to Singapore take?

Sample lead time is 5–10 days via express courier. Bulk orders take 20–30 days production plus approximately 7–10 days sea freight to Singapore (Tanjong Pagar / Pasir Panjang terminals). Total time from deposit to arrival is typically 30–45 days.

Can you provide GST invoices and Singapore customs documentation?

We issue standard export documentation including commercial invoice, packing list, Bill of Lading, Certificate of Origin (CO), and phytosanitary certificate where required. You handle Singapore customs clearance with your licensed forwarder — we provide all necessary paperwork.

Do you support custom OEM branding for Singapore retail chains?

Yes. We support custom Pantone color programs, OEM logo printing, barcode and QR labels, promotional insert cards, and retail-ready carton designs. We have supplied multiple Southeast Asia lifestyle and garden retail chains with private label planters.

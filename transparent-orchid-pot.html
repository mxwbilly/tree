Why transparent pots dominate orchid growing

  • Root health monitoring: growers see root color and moisture level without disturbing roots — green/silver roots indicate healthy vs dry status.
  • Photosynthesis boost: orchid roots contain chlorophyll and benefit from light exposure — clear pots improve root vitality.
  • Thailand orchid export: Thailand is the world's largest orchid exporter; transparent pots are standard for Dendrobium and Phalaenopsis trade packs.

Common buyer questions

  • PET vs PS? PET is more rigid and higher clarity; PS is lower cost. For orchid growers, PET is recommended. For seedling cups (single-use), PS is fine.
  • Do slots weaken the pot? No — reinforced slot rim design maintains structural integrity. Tested up to 2 kg plant + growing medium.
  • Can we print logos on clear pots? Yes — screen print or shrink sleeve label. MOQ 500 pcs for screen print.
  • Available with outer decorative pot? Yes — we can supply matched opaque outer cachepot to sit the clear orchid pot inside.

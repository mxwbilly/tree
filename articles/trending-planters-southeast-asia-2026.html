June 2026  ·  Vietnam · Thailand · Indonesia · Malaysia · Singapore · Philippines

Google Trends data for the Southeast Asian garden and horticulture market reveals clear divergence between countries. Importers sourcing from China need to match their buying plan to what is actually gaining traction in their local market — not a one-size-fits-all catalogue.

Singapore & Malaysia: Urban Premium

Self-watering ceramic planters score 74 in Singapore and 66 in Malaysia — the highest of any category in either market. Balcony planter boxes are equally strong (SG 78, MY 63). These two markets are driven by apartment living, gift retail, and chain garden centres that need premium, low-maintenance products.

  • Top picks: Self-watering ceramic planters, balcony planter boxes
  • Buyer type: Garden centre chains, lifestyle retailers, interior gifting brands
  • Key requirements: Retail packaging, OEM colour programmes, water-level display options

Vietnam & Indonesia: Agriculture & Eco Volume

Nursery trays score 68 (VN) and 72 (ID), reflecting the strong agricultural and propagation supply chains in both countries. Vietnam also indexes high for bamboo fiber planters (58) and terracotta pots (61), signalling demand for eco-retail alongside the grower supply segment.

  • Top picks: Stackable nursery trays, bamboo fiber planters, terracotta pots
  • Buyer type: Agri-supply distributors, garden hypermarkets, eco-retail chains
  • Key requirements: Bulk MOQ, stack-efficient shipping, biodegradable material options

Thailand: Lifestyle & Traditional Dual Demand

Thailand shows balanced demand across categories: terracotta (65), self-watering (54), and balcony planters (56). This reflects a mix of traditional clay-pot culture and a growing urban lifestyle retail segment. Importers in Bangkok serve both channels simultaneously.

  • Top picks: Terracotta pots, self-watering planters, balcony boxes
  • Buyer type: Mixed — traditional market distributors and modern trade retail
  • Key requirements: Classic finishes alongside contemporary colourways

Philippines: Nursery & Hanging Planters

The Philippines indexes strongest for nursery trays (56) and hanging coir baskets (55), with terracotta also holding steady at 53. The tropical climate and strong home gardening culture support year-round demand for practical propagation supplies and decorative hanging planters.

  • Top picks: Nursery tray sets, hanging coir baskets, terracotta pots
  • Buyer type: Nurseries, garden supply retailers, home decor importers
  • Key requirements: Affordable MOQ tiers, natural materials, durable outdoor finishes

How to Use This Data in Your Buying Plan

The score gap between markets is the key signal. A product that scores 74 in Singapore and 35 in the Philippines should not be bought in the same volume for both markets. Use the trend score to weight your initial trial orders: high-score categories get the first container test, low-score categories get held for a second season unless a specific retail partnership justifies the risk.
